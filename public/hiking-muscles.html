Which muscles work on a hike?

follow the glow: the hotter the color, the harder the muscle works

Flat trail

Every step uses the calves, quadriceps, hamstrings and glutes.

Uphill

  • Glutes - the main engine that drives you up
  • Quadriceps - straighten the knee to lift you
  • Calves - push off on every step
  • Hip flexors - lift the knee to the next step

Downhill

  • Quadriceps - work as brakes, that's why they ache the next day
  • Shins (tibialis) - control how the foot lands
  • Hamstrings - stabilize the knee

Core & upper body

  • Abs & obliques - keep you balanced on rocky ground
  • Lower back - supports the backpack's weight
  • Shoulders & traps - carry the backpack straps
  • Arms & triceps - push on the trekking poles

Tip: stretch your calves, quads and hips after the hike.
